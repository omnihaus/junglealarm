# Jungle Alarm Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Jungle Alarm is made by Omnihaus ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **we don't collect your data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't run any servers that receive your information. What the app stores stays on your iPhone or in your own iCloud account.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your alarm and settings:** wake-up time, alarm days, chosen animal, move and rep counts, and similar preferences.
- **Your mornings:** the date and time you got up, how long it took, which move you did, and your roar score. This powers your streak, calendar and personal bests.
- **Sleep from Apple Health (optional):** if you turn on "Show my sleep", the app reads how long you slept last night from Apple Health and shows it next to that morning. It only reads; it never writes to Health. Sleep data stays on your iPhone. It is not included in your iCloud backup and is never shared with your pack or anyone else.

## Microphone and camera

- **Microphone:** used only while you roar to turn off the alarm or test your roar. The sound is analysed live on your iPhone to measure the roar. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
- **Camera:** used only if you choose camera counting for your wake-up move. The image is analysed live on your iPhone to count reps. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

## iCloud

- **Backup:** your settings and mornings (without Apple Health data) are backed up to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so a new iPhone picks up where you left off. We can't see this data.
- **Packs (optional):** if you start or join a pack, Jungle Alarm uses Apple's CloudKit sharing to share a small set of information with the people in that pack only:
  - the name you choose for the pack to call you
  - your next alarm time, the time you last got up and how long it took
  - your streak, mornings won this week and last week, and your average wake-up time
  - the weekly stakes you set or join, who won and lost them, and whether they were settled

  This lives in the iCloud account of the person who started the pack and is visible to pack members. We can't see it. Leaving the pack removes your information from it, and closing a pack you started deletes it for everyone.
- **Invites:** when you challenge a friend, the app creates an iCloud share link. You choose who to send it to and through which app.

## Notifications

The app uses notifications for:
- your bedtime check
- your weekly recap
- pack activity, such as a friend joining or opting in to a stake
- if you haven't started your trial, up to 6 reminders over the next month

You choose whether to allow notifications. You can turn the trial reminders off in the app, and all notifications off in your iPhone's Settings.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active.

## Children

Jungle Alarm is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Deleting your information

- **Settings → Reset everything** erases your mornings, streak and settings from the app and from its iCloud backup.
- **Leave** or **Close pack** on the Pack tab removes your pack information.
- Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. You can also manage iCloud data in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
