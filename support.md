# Jungle Alarm Support

Questions, problems or feedback: **[your support email]**.

## Common questions

**How do I cancel or change my subscription?**
On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Jungle Alarm. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged.

**I bought Pro but the app is locked.**
Open the paywall and tap **Restore**. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

**My alarm didn't ring.**
- Check the alarm is **On** on the Alarm tab and today is one of your alarm days.
- Allow alarms for Jungle Alarm in Settings → Jungle Alarm.
- Keep your iPhone charged overnight.

**Why can't I just turn the alarm off?**
That's the point. The alarm only stops when you're out of bed and have done your move. Pressing the side or volume button quiets it for a minute, then it rings again.

**My moves aren't counting.**
Try **Practice the moves** on the Alarm tab to see how to hold the phone. You can switch between motion and camera counting, and lower your rep counts, in Settings.

**Packs aren't working.**
Packs use iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on your iPhone, and that iCloud is on for Jungle Alarm.

**How do I delete my data?**
In the app, go to Settings → Reset everything. To leave a pack, use **Leave** on the Pack tab. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for details.
